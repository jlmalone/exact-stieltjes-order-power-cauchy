# Exact Stieltjes Order of Power-Cauchy Minors

For $a>0$, strictly increasing spectra

\[
t_1<\cdots<t_n,\qquad x_1<\cdots<x_n,
\]

and a common shift $s>0$, consider

\[
K_{a,n}(s;\mathbf t,\mathbf x)
=\det\!\left[(s+t_i+x_j)^{-a}\right]_{i,j=1}^{n}.
\]

The central result identifies a positive compactly supported measure for this
determinant and proves that its exact generalized Stieltjes order is

\[
\boxed{n(a+n-1)}.
\]

More precisely,

\[
K_{a,n}(s;\mathbf t,\mathbf x)
=c_{a,n}\Delta(\mathbf t)\Delta(\mathbf x)
\mathbb E(s+Y)^{-n(a+n-1)},
\]

where

\[
c_{a,n}=\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{(a)_k}{k!}
\]

and $Y$ is an explicit sum of two unitary-orbital linear statistics of a
fixed-trace Laguerre random matrix. Its support, mean, and variance are
computed exactly.

The same order theorem extends to determinants of generalized Markov
transforms generated by strict Chebyshev systems and to their confluent
Wronskians. The representation also yields determinant bounds and a strict
Hausdorff-moment hierarchy for the normalized expansion at infinity.

## Main verification identities

The representation is checked against two independent limits:

\[
K_{a,n}(s)
\sim
c_{a,n}\Delta(\mathbf t)\Delta(\mathbf x)
s^{-n(a+n-1)}
\qquad(s\to\infty),
\]

and, for $a=1$, Cauchy's determinant formula gives

\[
\frac{K_{1,n}(s)}{\Delta(\mathbf t)\Delta(\mathbf x)}
=\prod_{i,j=1}^{n}(s+t_i+x_j)^{-1}.
\]

The second identity agrees with the uniform Dirichlet or Feynman-parameter
representation of the same generalized Stieltjes transform.
