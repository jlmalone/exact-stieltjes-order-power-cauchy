## Title

Exact Stieltjes Order of Power-Cauchy Minors via Fixed-Trace Unitary Orbits

## Abstract

For positive $a$ and two strictly ordered real spectra, consider the
determinant whose entries are $(s+t_i+x_j)^{-a}$. We prove that, as a function
of the common shift $s$, this minor is a generalized Stieltjes transform of
exact order $n(a+n-1)$. The representing probability law is explicit: it is
the sum of two independent unitary-orbital linear statistics conditioned on a
common fixed-trace Laguerre spectrum. We determine its full support, mean, and
variance. The representation yields sharp support-and-mean determinant bounds,
a quantitative variance refinement, and a strict Hausdorff-moment hierarchy
for the normalized expansion at infinity. Andréief's identity extends the
result to determinants of generalized Markov transforms generated by strict
Chebyshev systems and to their confluent Wronskians. The proof combines the
Gamma-Laplace representation, the HCIZ integral, and the Laguerre-Selberg
normalization. Independent double-alternant asymptotics confirm the exponent
and normalization.
